THE AXIVERSE

The world begins with a single creature.

Every creature in the Axiverse begins at the same starting point. From there, its journey is shaped by evolution, craft, and the choices made by its holder.

The Axiverse is an evolving collection built around five distinct levels of rarity. A creature does not begin as a legend. It begins small, at Level 1, and grows through the world one stage at a time.

The first form is Common. It is the beginning of every journey. From Common, a creature can progress into Uncommon, then Rare, Epic, and finally Legendary.

Each level represents a new stage in the creature's evolution. The higher the level, the further that creature has travelled through the Axiverse.

I
THE FIVE LEVELS

From the beginning to the final form.

Level 1 — Common.
The beginning. Every creature enters the Axiverse in its first form.

Level 2 — Uncommon.
A new form emerges. The creature has taken its first step beyond its original state.

Level 3 — Rare.
Greater potential awakens. The creature continues its progression toward a more advanced form.

Level 4 — Epic.
Power takes shape. The creature reaches an advanced stage of evolution.

Level 5 — Legendary.
The final evolution. The highest form within the Axiverse.

II
CRAFT & EVOLUTION

Nothing evolves without a beginning.

Evolution is built through Craft. Creatures can be combined to progress toward the next level, turning two creatures from the same stage into one creature of the stage above.

The journey starts with two Level 1 creatures. Combine them and the path continues toward Level 2. The same principle carries forward through the five levels of the Axiverse.

LV.1 + LV.1 → LV.2

III
THE JOURNEY

Summon. Evolve. Become.

The journey begins with a Summon. AXI is burned to bring a new creature into the Axiverse. From that first form, its evolution becomes part of the collection's story.

The goal is not simply to own a creature. It is to see how far that creature can progress — from Common to Legendary, from its first form to its final evolution.

And beyond evolution, the creatures can become part of the wider Axiverse ecosystem through staking, where NFT holders can earn USDG rewards.
